Meet the team behind your smile.

Our clinicians combine specialist training with a gentle, communication-first approach.

Dr. Humayun Aleem

Dental Surgeon

Dr.Humayun Aleem is a compassionate and highly skilled Dental Professional with over 20 years of clinical experience in providing advanced dental care. He specialises in Root Canal and General Dentistry. His approach combines modern dental techniques with gentle patient care ensuring a comfortable and stress-free experience for every patient.

At Asian Dental Clinic, Hoskote, Dr.Humayun Aleem is committed to delivering high quality dentistry, focussing on Root Canal treatments, Crowns and Bridges, Smile Designing, Dental Implants and Preventive Dental Care. Known for his patience, precision and transparent communication, he believes in educating patients about their oral health to enable long-term Dental Wellness.

Dr.Humayun Aleem has also been an active member of the Indian Dental Association and has won several accolades for his marvellous work and expert knowledge.

Dr.Humayun Aleem knows multiple languages so that he can communicate with patients with ease. He has worked in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia as well and has treated people around the world.

Dr.Ameena Tabassum

Dental Surgeon

Dr.Ameena Tabassum is an experienced dental practitioner known for her patient-centric approach and excellence with more than 18 years of clinical experience delivering high quality, evidence-based treatment for patients of all ages.

Dr.Ameena Tabassum is committed to delivering high quality dentistry focussing on Smile Designing, Dental Implants, Crowns and Bridges, Painless Laser Dentistry, Root Canals, General Dentistry, Clear Aligners and Preventive Dentistry.

Dr.Ameena Tabassum has advanced training in Cosmetic and Implant Dentistry and Orthodontic Aligners and educates patients about their oral health to enable long-term dental wellness.

Dr.Ameena Tabassum uses modern technology and quality materials, known for her precision and transparent communication with comfort for every patient. She has worked in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia as well and has treated people around the world.
